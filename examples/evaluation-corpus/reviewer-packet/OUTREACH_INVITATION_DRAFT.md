## Draft text

**Subject:** Optional independent technical review of a synthetic TrustWeave evaluation packet

Hello [reviewer name or role],

You are invited to consider an optional **45–60 minute offline technical review** of the TrustWeave synthetic evaluation packet at [exact repository commit/package version]. TrustWeave is a local-first, non-executing tool that produces deterministic review artifacts from supplied declarations and minimized local metadata. This exercise evaluates only the clarity and reproducibility of checked-in synthetic materials.

The packet does not ask you to test a live agent, server, MCP endpoint, target, customer environment, proprietary repository, real trace, message content, tool argument, credential, or personal account. Please do not provide any sensitive, proprietary, or unauthorized material.

If you choose to participate, the packet asks you to run the local corpus, inspect two supplied synthetic cases, and complete the structured feedback template. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time and may withdraw your response before [analysis cutoff] through [owner-approved private contact route]. The owner will state the intended use of responses before you begin: [anonymous aggregate only / other owner-approved use].

Before beginning, please confirm whether you were independent of authorship for the evaluated TrustWeave revision and whether you consent to the stated use of your response. The project will not describe a response as independent-review evidence unless those conditions, the synthetic-only boundary, and the fixed packet requirements are met.

The packet is available at [owner-approved repository URL or attached public-safe package]. The packet README, feedback template, evaluation charter, reviewer protocol, data-minimization policy, conflicts/limitations record, and status ledger explain the task and evidence boundaries.

Thank you for considering this optional technical review.
