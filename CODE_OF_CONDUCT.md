# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TrustWeave contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment. We welcome participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels, provided they engage constructively and responsibly.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to communicate with respect, give and receive technical feedback in good faith, focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than people, protect confidential information, and follow the project’s documented security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, publication of real credentials, unsafe instructions targeting third parties, or other conduct that makes collaboration unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when they involve security-sensitive information, or directly to the repository maintainer for conduct concerns. Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect contributors and users.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from widely used open-source community standards and is intentionally concise for the project’s current pre-release stage.
